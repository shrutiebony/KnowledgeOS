Hypertension in adults is common and often silent until a cardiovascular event. Guidelines distinguish office, home, and ambulatory measurements because white-coat and masked hypertension are not rare.

Lifestyle advice is not decoration: sodium, alcohol, weight, and activity change blood pressure in trials, though effect sizes are modest compared with combination pills. First-line drugs still cluster around ACE inhibitors, ARBs, calcium-channel blockers, and thiazides.
