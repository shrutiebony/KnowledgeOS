Asthma is variable airflow obstruction with a messy mix of inflammation, atopy, and triggers that do not respect clinic hours. Peak-flow diaries are unfashionable; inhaler technique remains the intervention that guidelines repeat because people still do it wrong.

Relievers versus controllers is the first fork. Over-reliance on short-acting beta agonists is a safety story, not a preference. Biologics exist for severe eosinophilic disease and are not a first prescription for a cold that wheezes.
